Book Sort: Color Puzzle is a calm color sort puzzle set in a pixel night library. Sort books by color onto wooden shelves.

Tap a shelf to lift the books on top. Tap another shelf to set them down. Books land only on an empty shelf, or on a shelf whose top book is the same color and still has room. Fill a shelf with one color to finish it. Clear every shelf to win.

What you will find

  • 209 levels, from a short lesson to longer evening puzzles.
  • Twelve book colors, each with its own cover mark.
  • Hidden books, wrapped in cloth until the books above them move.
  • Locked shelves that open once another shelf is finished.
  • A return cart, where one book can wait out of the way.
  • Undo and restart on every level, and a hint when you want the next step.
  • Stars for finishing close to the target number of moves.

Play without an account. Your stars stay on this device. If you like color sort puzzles, the rhythm will feel familiar: same color together, one shelf at a time.

Book Sort is on Google Play.

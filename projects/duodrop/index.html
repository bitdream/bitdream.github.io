Duo Drop is a juicy color-match puzzle. Drop linked twin gems, line up four of the same color, and free every jelly in the well.

Easy to start. One thumb. No tutorial wall. The first levels teach the move by letting you win.

How to play

  • Swipe to slide the twin gem.
  • Tap to turn it.
  • Flick down to drop.
  • Match four of a color to pop. Chains keep going.

Modes

  • Adventure — 12 flavor worlds, 240 levels. Sugar to Prime. One to three stars per clear.
  • Endless — no jellies to hunt. Survive as the drop speed climbs.
  • Daily — one shared board for everyone, once a day. Keep a streak.
  • VS AI — same opening, two wells. Race Blaze, Mint, and Wobble.

Play offline. No lives. No energy bar. Ads stay out of the well.

Xshots turns a public X post into a PNG image or a PDF you can save and share.

Paste a link from x.com or twitter.com, or share it from the browser or the X app. Xshots loads the public post and shows a preview. Nothing is exported until you choose a format.

A link anywhere in a thread opens the whole thread. Pick a template: X style, Clean, Social, Quote, or Paper. You can switch a dark theme, and show or hide the time and the engagement counts.

Export a long PNG, one PNG per post, a PDF with one post per page, or one long PDF page. Save the image to the gallery, or share the file. Videos embedded in the post can be saved to the gallery too.

Public posts only. Replies from other people are not included. Xshots does not post, like, or reply on X.

Xshots is on Google Play.

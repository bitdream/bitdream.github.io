1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Jelly Word Merge (“the App”) handles information when you use the Android version distributed on Google Play. We aim to collect as little as necessary to run the game, show ads (unless removed), and process optional in-app purchases.

2. Information we store on your device

The App stores gameplay data locally on your device (not on our own servers), including:

  • Level progress, unlocked levels, and play / clear history
  • Settings such as music, sound effects, and vibration preferences
  • Hint balance and whether ads have been removed via purchase

Uninstalling the App or clearing app data may erase this local information.

3. Advertising

The App may show advertisements provided by Google AdMob (and Google’s advertising partners), including rewarded ads (for example, hints or extra moves) and interstitial ads.

Advertising-related data may include:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information needed to serve and measure ads
  • Approximate location / IP-derived region (as processed by Google)
  • Ad interaction events

Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent for personalized ads. You can also reset or limit your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Google’s policies and information: Google Privacy Policy, AdMob help.

You may purchase Remove Ads in the App shop. After a successful purchase, ads are disabled while the purchase remains valid on your Google Play account / device restore path.

4. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads and Hints Pack) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Google handles payment processing under its own terms and privacy policy.

5. App functionality services

  • Google Play In-App Review — may be used when you tap Rate, so you can leave feedback in Play.
  • Internet access — required for ads, purchases, and review prompts.
  • Vibration — used only for optional haptic feedback if enabled in Settings.

6. Data we do not collect ourselves

We do not require you to create an account. We do not intentionally collect:

  • Your name, email, or phone number inside the game
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera content
  • Chat messages or user-generated social posts

We do not operate our own analytics backend for this App. Advertising and billing partners may process data as described in their policies.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience word puzzle game. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party ads, contact us and also review parental controls / ad settings on the device.

8. Data retention & security

Local game data remains on your device until you clear it or uninstall the App. Advertising and purchase records may be retained by Google according to their policies. We take reasonable steps appropriate for a small mobile game, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Your choices

  • Turn off music / SFX / vibration in the App Settings
  • Reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android settings (Advertising / Ads)
  • Respond to the consent form (UMP) where shown
  • Purchase Remove Ads to stop ad serving in the App
  • Uninstall the App at any time

10. International processing

Google and its partners may process data on servers outside your country. Where consent or other legal bases are required, they are handled through the applicable Google Play / AdMob mechanisms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
